# HR Analytics Dashboard 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard provides insights into employee attrition and workforce demographics to help HR teams understand employee turnover patterns and identify areas for improvement. It presents key HR metrics through KPI cards, charts, and job-role breakdowns in an interactive dashboard.

The analysis focuses on employee count, attrition rate, age, salary, education, gender, job roles, and years at the company.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Employees:** 1,470
- **Employees Who Left:** 237
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.1%
- **Average Employee Age:** 37 years
- **Average Salary:** 6.5K (as displayed on the dashboard)
- **Average Years at Company:** 7
- **Attrition by Gender:** 140 male employees and 79 female employees

## 🔍 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Attrition by Education
Analyzes employee attrition across educational backgrounds, including Life Sciences, Medical, Marketing, and Technical Degree categories.

### 2. Attrition by Age
Compares attrition across age groups to identify the age ranges with higher employee turnover.

### 3. Attrition by Job Role
Highlights attrition across job positions, including Laboratory Technicians, Sales Executives, Research Scientists, and Sales Representatives.

### 4. Attrition by Salary
Examines employee attrition across salary bands to explore the relationship between compensation levels and employee turnover.

### 5. Attrition by Years at Company
Visualizes attrition according to employee tenure, helping identify service periods where employees may be more likely to leave.

### 6. Job Role and Tenure Breakdown
Provides a detailed table of attrition by job role and years of service, supporting comparisons across employee groups.

### 7. Workforce Demographics
Summarizes employee age, gender, education, and other workforce characteristics to provide a broader view of employee composition.
